## 🎮 Game Mechanics Summary

### Core Loop
1. Draw a card
2. Play a card (build stacks, play events)
3. Discard to 7 cards

### Winning Condition
- Complete 2 Research Stacks
- Highest total score wins

### Scoring System
- Paradigm Chain Bonus (-5 to +10 points)
- Ontology Special Effects
- Theory Bonus (0 to +7 points)
- Object-of-Study Affinity (+2 points)
- Ethical Penalty (-1 per method)
- Cutting-Edge Bonus (+3 points)
- Final Awards (+5 points each)

### Strategic Depth
- Multiple viable strategies
- Epistemology choice matters
- Trade-offs (Reproducibility vs Sensibility)
- Resource management (hand, discard)
- Risk/reward (extreme methods)

## 💡 Key Design Decisions

### 1. Epistemology-Centric Design
Methods synergize with Epistemologies (not Ontologies), making epistemological choice the core strategic decision.

### 2. Educational First
Every Method card teaches something. Pros/cons/use cases provide real research insight.

### 3. Balanced Scoring
Multiple paths to victory:
- Coherent paradigms (methodological rigor)
- High-value theories (specialization)
- Ontology effects (strategic bonuses)
- Final awards (long-term planning)

### 4. Methodological Extremes Rule
Methods with very high or very low scores require extra cost, representing the real-world challenge of extreme approaches.

### 5. Secret Ontology
Players' first Ontology is secret, creating asymmetric starting positions and replayability.
