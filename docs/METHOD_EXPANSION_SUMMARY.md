# Method Card Expansion Summary

**Date**: 2025-11-06
**Expansion**: Method Cards 47 → 82 (+35 new methods)
**Total Cards**: 118 → 153 (+35 cards)

---

## Overview

The Methodology Match deck has been expanded to provide comprehensive coverage of research methods across:
- Arts & Design Research
- Art History
- Social Sciences
- Digital Humanities
- Archival & Historical Methods
- Qualitative Research
- Quantitative Research

This expansion ensures the game reflects the full spectrum of methods used in practice research, arts, humanities, design, art history, games research, and social sciences.

## NEW METHODS ADDED (35 Total)

### Design Research Methods (11 new)
1. **Usability Testing** - User testing for interface evaluation
2. **A/B Testing** - Comparing two versions experimentally
3. **Card Sorting** - Understanding mental models through categorization
4. **Diary Study** - Participants document experiences over time
5. **Journey Mapping** - Visualizing user experience over time
6. **Prototyping as Method** - Using prototypes as research tools
7. **Contextual Inquiry** - Observing users in natural environments
8. **Think-Aloud Protocol** - Users verbalize thoughts during tasks
9. **Co-Design** - Collaborative design with stakeholders
10. **Cultural Probes** - Kits for documenting lived experience (already existed, description added)
11. **Data Visualization** - Creating visual representations of data

### Art History Methods (6 new)
12. **Iconographic Analysis** - Studying symbolism in visual art
13. **Stylistic Analysis** - Analyzing formal visual qualities
14. **Comparative Visual Analysis** - Systematic artwork comparison
15. **Connoisseurship** - Expert judgment for attribution
16. **Provenance Research** - Tracing artwork ownership history
17. **Technical Art History** - Scientific analysis of artworks

### Social Science Quantitative (8 new)
18. **Social Network Analysis** - Mapping relationships and flows
19. **Q-Methodology** - Studying subjective viewpoints
20. **Meta-Analysis** - Statistical synthesis of multiple studies
21. **Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT)** - Gold standard experimental design
22. **Regression Analysis** - Modeling variable relationships
23. **Factor Analysis** - Identifying underlying data structures
24. **Delphi Method** - Expert consensus technique
25. **Quasi-Experimental Design** - Experimental design without full randomization

### Qualitative Methods (4 new)
26. **Conversation Analysis** - Micro-analysis of talk-in-interaction
27. **Life History Method** - In-depth biographical research
28. **Photo Voice** - Participatory photography method
29. **Video Analysis** - Systematic analysis of video recordings

### Digital/Computational Methods (4 new)
30. **Close Reading** - Careful text interpretation (Humanities)
31. **Distant Reading** - Computational analysis of large text corpora
32. **Topic Modeling** - Discovering abstract topics algorithmically
33. **GIS/Spatial Analysis** - Geographic information systems
34. **Sentiment Analysis** - Computational emotional tone analysis

### Cross-Cutting Methods (2 new)
35. **Reception Studies** - How audiences interpret texts/artworks
36. **Systematic Review** - Comprehensive structured review

## DESCRIPTIONS ADDED TO ALL 82 METHODS

Every Method card now includes:
- **Clear 1-2 sentence description** explaining what the method is
- **Epistemology alignment** showing which paradigms it fits
- **Complete statistics** (Ethical Risk, Reproducibility, Sensibility, Novelty)

### Example Before:
```csv
Method,Ethnography,,"Interpretivism, Critical",,7,3,8,5
```

### Example After:
```csv
Method,Ethnography,"Immersive fieldwork where the researcher participates in and observes a community or culture over time, producing rich contextual understanding.","Interpretivism, Critical",,7,3,8,5
```
