## The Big Issues (Must Fix)

### 1️⃣ Cards Don't Show Scoring ⚠️ CRITICAL

**Problem**: Players can't calculate scores from cards alone.

**Example**: Method card shows "Ethical Risk: 7" but doesn't say this means "-1 point"

**Solution**: Add scoring text/icons to all cards

---

### 2️⃣ Method Cards Have No Descriptions ⚠️ CRITICAL

**Problem**: Cards just show names and numbers.

**Current**:
```
Method: Phenomenology
Alignment: Embodied
Stats: 6, 3, 9, 5
```

**Needed**:
```
Method: Phenomenology
Description: A qualitative method exploring lived experience
and consciousness through in-depth interviews.
Alignment: Embodied, Interpretivism
[Scoring info and stats]
```

**Solution**: All 47 Method descriptions provided in full review (Section 8)

---

### 3️⃣ Only 3 Ontology Cards ⚠️ CRITICAL

**Problem**:
- Game works for exactly 3 players
- 2-player game: limited variety
- 4-player game: IMPOSSIBLE

**Current Ontologies**:
- Realism
- Relativism
- Constructivism

**Solution**: Add 2 more (Critical Realism, Relational Ontology) → 5 total

**Bonus Issue**: Ontology cards have "Gameplay Effects" that aren't in the rules!

---

### 4️⃣ Synergy Names Don't Match ⚠️ MEDIUM

**Problem**: Epistemology cards list synergy methods with different names than actual Method cards.

**Examples**:
- Positivism says "Surveys" → Method card is "Survey (Quantitative)"
- Positivism says "Experiments" → Method card is "Experimental Design"
- Feminist Epistemology says "Zine-Making" → NO CARD EXISTS

**Solution**: Standardize all names and remove non-existent cards from lists

---

### 5️⃣ Object-of-Study Scoring is Guesswork ⚠️ MEDIUM

**Problem**: Rules say "+2 if thematically aligned" but don't define what that means.

**Solution**: Add keywords to all cards
- Object: "Digital Media" → Keywords: #Digital, #Contemporary, #Social
- Method: "Netnography" → Keywords: #Digital, #Ethnographic
- Match keywords = +2 points (objective!)
