# Method Card Expansion Research

## Current Coverage Analysis (47 Methods)

### What We Have:
- **Quantitative**: Experimental Design, Survey, Statistical Analysis, Content Analysis, Social Media Mining, Corpus Linguistics
- **Qualitative**: Ethnography, Interview, Case Study, Thematic Analysis, Grounded Theory, Narrative Inquiry, Oral History
- **Arts/Creative**: Practice-Based Research, Drawing as Inquiry, Creative Writing as Inquiry, Performance Ethnography, Exhibition-as-Research
- **Critical**: Discourse Analysis, Critical Discourse Analysis, Action Research, Participatory Methods
- **Embodied**: Walking Interview, Soundwalk Method, Embodied Mapping, Phenomenology, Sensory Ethnography
- **Digital**: Netnography, Digital Humanities Methods, VR Ethnography, Social Media Mining
- **Visual/Cultural**: Film Analysis, Visual Analysis, Material Culture Analysis, Artefact Analysis
- **Historical**: Archival Research, Historical Method, Documentary Analysis, Biographical Method
- **Design**: Speculative Design, Cultural Probes

### Major Gaps Identified:

## 1. DESIGN RESEARCH METHODS (Missing ~10)
- **Usability Testing**: Testing products/interfaces with users
- **A/B Testing**: Comparing two versions to determine which performs better
- **Card Sorting**: Understanding mental models through categorization
- **Diary Study**: Participants document experiences over time
- **Journey Mapping**: Visualizing user experience over time
- **Service Design Research**: Studying service touchpoints and experiences
- **Prototyping as Method**: Using prototypes to explore and test ideas
- **Contextual Inquiry**: Observing users in their natural environment
- **Think-Aloud Protocol**: Users verbalize thoughts while performing tasks
- **Design Workshops**: Facilitated collaborative design sessions
- **Heuristic Evaluation**: Expert review against usability principles

## 2. ART HISTORY METHODS (Missing ~6)
- **Iconographic Analysis**: Studying symbolism and meaning in visual art
- **Stylistic Analysis**: Analyzing formal visual qualities (line, color, composition)
- **Connoisseurship**: Expert judgment of attribution, quality, authenticity
- **Provenance Research**: Tracing ownership and movement of artworks
- **Technical Art History**: Scientific analysis of materials and techniques
- **Comparative Visual Analysis**: Systematic comparison of artworks

## 3. SOCIAL SCIENCE QUANTITATIVE (Missing ~10)
- **Q-Methodology**: Studying subjective viewpoints through factor analysis
- **Social Network Analysis**: Mapping and measuring relationships
- **Meta-Analysis**: Statistical synthesis of multiple studies
- **Systematic Review**: Comprehensive review of all available evidence
- **Regression Analysis**: Modeling relationships between variables
- **Factor Analysis**: Identifying underlying structures in data
- **Cross-Sectional Study**: Observing many subjects at one point in time
- **Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT)**: Gold standard experimental design
- **Quasi-Experimental Design**: Experimental design without full randomization
- **Delphi Method**: Structured expert consensus technique
- **Conjoint Analysis**: Understanding preference trade-offs

## 4. HUMANITIES METHODS (Missing ~6)
- **Close Reading**: Careful, sustained interpretation of text
- **Distant Reading**: Computational analysis of large text corpora
- **Iconology**: Interpreting cultural significance of images (beyond iconography)
- **Reception Studies**: How audiences/readers interpret texts
- **Philology**: Historical study of language and texts
- **Book History/Bibliography**: Material study of books as objects

## 5. QUALITATIVE METHODS (Missing ~8)
- **Conversation Analysis**: Micro-analysis of talk-in-interaction
- **Life History Method**: In-depth biographical research
- **Photo Voice/Photo Elicitation**: Using photographs to elicit discussion (we have Photo Elicitation mentioned but not as card)
- **Video Analysis**: Systematic analysis of video data
- **Cognitive Mapping**: Visualizing mental representations
- **Repertory Grid**: Eliciting personal constructs
- **Focus Group (Qualitative)**: We have Focus Groups but not specified
- **Participant Observation**: Immersive observation (might be covered by Ethnography)

## 6. DIGITAL/COMPUTATIONAL METHODS (Missing ~7)
- **Topic Modeling**: Discovering abstract topics in text collections
- **Network Analysis**: Analyzing network structures (social, textual, conceptual)
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Computational analysis of emotional tone
- **Data Visualization**: Creating visual representations of data
- **GIS/Spatial Analysis**: Geographic information systems for spatial data
- **Web Scraping/Analytics**: Collecting and analyzing web data
- **Eye Tracking**: Recording eye movements to study attention

## 7. ARCHIVAL/HISTORICAL SPECIALISTS (Missing ~4)
- **Paleography**: Study of historical handwriting
- **Diplomatic Analysis**: Critical analysis of document authenticity and form
- **Prosopography**: Collective biography/study of groups
- **Codicology**: Study of books as physical objects (manuscripts)

## 8. COLLABORATIVE/PARTICIPATORY (Missing ~3)
- **Community-Based Participatory Research (CBPR)**: Research with community as partners
- **Co-Design**: Collaborative design with stakeholders
- **Participatory Video**: Communities create video to document their lives
