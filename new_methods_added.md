## New Methods

### 1. Experiments
**Synergies**: Positivism, Objectivism

**Scores**:
- Ethical Risk: 6 (moderate - experiments may involve participants, manipulation, controlled interventions)
- Reproducibility: 9 (very high - controlled conditions, standardized protocols)
- Sensibility: 2 (low - focuses on variables and causation, not deep interpretation)
- Novelty: 5 (standard research method, but can incorporate innovative designs)

**Rationale**:
- Classic positivist method for testing hypotheses
- High reproducibility due to controlled conditions
- Referenced in Positivism epistemology synergies
- Distinct from "Experimental Design" which is about the design framework

---

### 2. Forensic Aesthetics
**Synergies**: Critical Epistemology, Aesthetic Epistemology

**Scores**:
- Ethical Risk: 5 (moderate - often involves sensitive political materials, power dynamics)
- Reproducibility: 4 (moderate-low - interpretive analysis of evidence, but evidence-based)
- Sensibility: 8 (high - requires deep critical reading of aesthetic and material evidence)
- Novelty: 8 (emerging critical method, cutting-edge in critical humanities)

**Rationale**:
- Method developed by Eyal Weizman and Forensic Architecture team
- Combines critical theory with aesthetic analysis of evidence
- Referenced in Critical Epistemology synergies
- Fits arts/humanities/design focus of the game

---

### 3. Statistical Modelling
**Synergies**: Positivism, Objectivism

**Scores**:
- Ethical Risk: 2 (low - primarily working with data, mathematical models)
- Reproducibility: 9 (very high - mathematical, algorithmic, replicable)
- Sensibility: 1 (very low - quantitative, no interpretive depth)
- Novelty: 7 (can be cutting-edge with machine learning, advanced techniques)

**Rationale**:
- Core quantitative method for pattern detection and prediction
- Highest reproducibility, lowest sensibility (pure quant)
- Referenced in Positivism epistemology synergies
- Triggers "Methodological Extremes" rule (Sensibility ≤ 2)

---

### 4. Zine-Making
**Synergies**: Feminist Epistemology, Critical Epistemology

**Scores**:
- Ethical Risk: 4 (moderate - collaborative work, representation, participant voice)
- Reproducibility: 2 (low - creative practice, unique outputs, DIY ethos)
- Sensibility: 8 (high - personal expression, political awareness, embodied knowledge)
- Novelty: 7 (innovative feminist DIY practice, established but fresh)

**Rationale**:
- Feminist participatory research method
- Combines creative practice with critical consciousness-raising
- Referenced in Feminist Epistemology synergies
- Triggers "Methodological Extremes" rule (Reproducibility ≤ 2)
