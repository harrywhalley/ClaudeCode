## [v1.0] - 2025-11-06 - Initial Complete Version

### Added
- Complete rulebook with all mechanics
- 116 cards total:
  - 3 Ontology cards with special effects
  - 10 Epistemology cards (added New Materialism)
  - 21 Theory/Framework cards (added Practice Theory)
  - 47 Method cards with educational content
  - 20 Object-of-Study cards
  - 15 Event cards
- Educational descriptions for all Method cards
- Coherent synergy system across all cards

### Fixed
- All synergy naming inconsistencies (27 methods corrected)
- Ontology effects now documented in rulebook
- Card counts corrected throughout

### Rules
- Ontology Special Effects:
  - Realism: +2 points for Reproducibility ≥7 methods
  - Relativism: No Incompatible penalty, capped at Mixed Paradigm
  - Constructivism: Draw card for Sensibility ≥7 methods
- Methodological Extremes: Extra discard for Repro/Sensibility ≤2
- Coherent Paradigm: +10 points
- Incompatible Paradigm: -5 points
- Mixed Paradigm: +3 points

## Change Categories

Use these categories to organize changes:

### Added
New features, cards, rules, or mechanics that didn't exist before.

### Changed
Modifications to existing elements without removing them.

### Fixed
Bug fixes, corrections, clarifications of existing content.

### Removed
Elements that have been cut from the game.

### Balanced
Changes specifically made for game balance (may overlap with Changed).

### Clarified
Rules or card text made clearer without functional change.

## Version Numbering

**Major version** (X.0): Significant rule changes or major card overhauls
- Example: v1.0 → v2.0 (new scoring system)

**Minor version** (1.X): Small rule tweaks, card balance adjustments
- Example: v1.0 → v1.1 (adjusted Ontology bonuses)

**Patch version** (1.0.X): Typos, clarifications, no gameplay impact
- Example: v1.0.0 → v1.0.1 (fixed typo on Ethnography card)

## Change Documentation Template

When documenting a change, include:

**What**: Exact change made
**Why**: Reason for change (issue #, playtest feedback, balance)
**Impact**: What this affects in gameplay
**Testing**: How this was validated

Example:
```
### Changed
- Realism Ontology bonus increased from +2 to +3 points

**Why**: Playtest sessions #3, #4, #5 showed Realism consistently underperformed compared to other Ontologies (Issues #012, #013)

**Impact**: Makes quantitative methods more viable as a winning strategy, brings Realism in line with other Ontologies

**Testing**: Validated in sessions #6, #7 - Realism now competitive
```
