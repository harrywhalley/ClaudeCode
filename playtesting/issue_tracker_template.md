## Issue Template

When logging a new issue, include:

**ID**: Sequential number (001, 002, 003...)
**Date**: Date discovered
**Category**:
- Rules Clarity
- Card Text
- Balance
- Strategy
- Educational Value
- Physical Design
- Scoring
- Turn Structure
- Edge Case
- Other

**Description**:
- What happened?
- When does it occur?
- Which cards/rules are involved?
- Frequency (every game / sometimes / rare)

**Severity**:
- **Critical**: Breaks the game, makes it unplayable
- **High**: Major confusion or frustration, significantly impacts experience
- **Medium**: Noticeable issue but game is still playable
- **Low**: Minor polish, cosmetic, or "nice to have"

**Proposed Fix**:
- Specific change to rule or card
- Alternative solutions
- Trade-offs to consider

**Playtest Source**: Which session(s) identified this?

## Issue Categories Explained

### Rules Clarity
Issues where rules are ambiguous, confusing, or missing important details.

*Examples*:
- "Players weren't sure when to score their stack"
- "Confusion about whether Event cards are mandatory"
- "Edge case not covered: what if draw pile runs out?"

### Card Text
Issues with wording, readability, or information on specific cards.

*Examples*:
- "Autoethnography description too long to read"
- "Synergies not clearly marked on Epistemology cards"
- "Typo on Feminist Epistemology card"

### Balance
Issues where certain cards, strategies, or game elements are too strong/weak.

*Examples*:
- "Realism Ontology bonus too weak compared to others"
- "Highly specialized Theories (7 points) not worth it"
- "Positivism has too many Method synergies"

### Strategy
Issues with strategic depth, decision-making, or dominant strategies.

*Examples*:
- "Only one winning strategy emerged"
- "No meaningful decisions during turns"
- "Optimal play is too obvious"

### Educational Value
Issues with learning outcomes or academic accuracy.

*Examples*:
- "Players didn't learn anything new about methods"
- "Card descriptions too academic/inaccessible"
- "Inaccurate representation of Phenomenology"

### Physical Design
Issues with card layout, readability, or physical components.

*Examples*:
- "Text too small to read at arm's length"
- "Can't distinguish card types at a glance"
- "Need color-coding or icons"

### Scoring
Issues with point calculations or scoring mechanics.

*Examples*:
- "Scoring too complicated to calculate"
- "Players forgot to apply Ontology bonuses"
- "Final awards had no impact on winner"

### Turn Structure
Issues with flow of play, turn order, or game phases.

*Examples*:
- "Turns took too long"
- "Draw-play-discard order felt awkward"
- "7-card hand limit too restrictive"
