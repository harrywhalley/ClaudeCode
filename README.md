# Methodology Match

A card game for arts, design, and humanities PhD researchers. Build coherent research projects by aligning methodologies and navigating academic life.

## About the Game

**Methodology Match** is a strategic card game where players construct Research Stacks by combining Ontologies, Epistemologies, Theories, and Methods. Score points through methodological coherence while dealing with the challenges of academic research.

**Players:** 2-4
**Play Time:** 30-45 minutes
**Complexity:** Medium

## Quick Start

1. Read the complete rules in `docs/rules_v1.md`
2. Each player receives 1 secret Ontology card
3. Reveal 1 shared Object-of-Study card
4. Build 2 Research Stacks to win
5. Score points through paradigm coherence and methodological alignment

## Winning the Game

The player with the highest score wins. Points come from:
- **Coherent Paradigm** (+10): Ontology and Epistemology align
- **Theory Bonuses** (+3 to +7): Theories matching your Epistemology
- **Object Affinity** (+2): Thematic fit with research topic
- **Cutting-Edge Bonus** (+3): High novelty methods
- **Final Awards** (+5 each): Highest Reproducibility or Sensibility scores
