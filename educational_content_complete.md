# Educational Content Added to All Method Cards

**Date**: 2025-11-06
**Status**: COMPLETE ✓

All 47 Method cards now include educational content suitable for physical card printing.

## Content Format

Each Method card now includes:

1. **Brief Description** (1-2 sentences): What the method is and does
2. **Pros** (3 bullet points): Key strengths and advantages
3. **Cons** (3 bullet points): Limitations and challenges
4. **Use** (2-3 examples): Typical research applications

## Content Characteristics

- **Concise**: Designed for limited card space
- **Academic**: Written for PhD researchers in arts/design/humanities
- **Practical**: Focuses on real-world application
- **Balanced**: Acknowledges both strengths and limitations
- **Accessible**: Clear language without oversimplification

## Example Format

```
Method,Ethnography,"Immersive fieldwork involving participant observation to understand cultural practices and meanings. Pros: Cultural depth; contextual richness; emic perspective. Cons: Time-intensive; researcher presence effects; ethical complexity. Use: Community studies; subcultural research; organizational culture."
```

## Content Examples

### Highly Quantitative Method
**Statistical Modelling**: "Building mathematical models to represent relationships between variables and predict outcomes. Pros: Predictive power; handles complexity; testable. Cons: Model assumptions; interpretability; data requirements. Use: Predictive analysis; pattern modeling; computational humanities."

### Highly Qualitative Method
**Autoethnography**: "Researcher uses personal experience as primary data to analyze cultural phenomena. Pros: Insider perspective; challenges objectivity myths; accessible writing. Cons: Difficult to generalize; vulnerable self-exposure; criticized as narcissistic. Use: Identity studies; reflexive practice research; embodied experience."

### Practice-Based Method
**Exhibition-as-Research**: "Using curatorial practice and exhibition-making as a mode of scholarly inquiry and argument. Pros: Public-facing research; spatial arguments; multi-modal. Cons: Resource-intensive; institutional gatekeeping; documentation challenges. Use: Curatorial research; practice-led inquiry; museum studies."

### Critical Method
**Forensic Aesthetics**: "Critical investigation using aesthetic analysis of material evidence to challenge official narratives. Pros: Visual evidence; counter-narratives; spatial reconstruction. Cons: Requires technical expertise; contested authority; resource-intensive. Use: Human rights investigation; spatial analysis; architectural evidence."
