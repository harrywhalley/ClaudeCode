# Rulebook v2 Changes Summary

**Date**: 2025-11-06
**Updated**: rules_v1.md → rules_v2 (same file, updated content)

## Major Changes

### 1. Updated Card Counts (Section 2)

**OLD:**
```
The full game consists of 114 cards:
- 3 Ontology Cards
- 9 Epistemology Cards
- 20 Theory/Framework Cards
- 47 Method Cards
- 20 Object-of-Study Cards
- 15 unique Event Cards
```

**NEW:**
```
The full game consists of 118 cards:
- 3 Ontology Cards
- 10 Epistemology Cards (+1 New Materialism)
- 21 Theory/Framework Cards (+1 Practice Theory)
- 47 Method Cards
- 20 Object-of-Study Cards
- 15 unique Event Cards
```

### 2. Added Card Type Details (New Section 2.1)

Added comprehensive listing of all card types:

**Ontology Cards (3):**
- Lists all 3 ontologies

**Epistemology Cards (10):**
- Lists all 10 epistemologies including the new New Materialism

**Theory/Framework Cards (21):**
- Organized by point tier (Highly Specialized 7pts, Standard 5pts, Flexible 3pts)
- Lists all 21 theories including the new Practice Theory

### 3. Added Ontology Special Effects (New Section 4.1)

Added detailed gameplay effects for each Ontology card:

**Realism:**
- Gain +2 bonus points if stack includes Method with Reproducibility ≥7

**Relativism:**
- May contain contradictory methods
- Cannot earn Coherent Paradigm bonus
- Never suffers Incompatible Paradigm penalty
- Always receives Mixed Paradigm (+3 points)

**Constructivism:**
- When stack completes with Method having Sensibility ≥7, draw 1 additional card

### 4. Clarified Incompatibility Rules (Section 7.1)

**Added clarification:**
- Emphasized that Epistemology cards list compatible/incompatible Ontologies
- Added note that Method cards list which Epistemologies they synergize with
- Clarified that players should check both Epistemology and Method cards when building stacks

**OLD:**
> Incompatible Paradigm (-5 Points): Applied if your stack's Ontology and Epistemology are explicitly listed as incompatible on the cards.

**NEW:**
> Incompatible Paradigm (-5 Points): Applied if your stack's Ontology and Epistemology are explicitly listed as incompatible on the cards. Check your Epistemology card to see which Ontologies it lists as "Incompatible" (if any).

Plus added note about checking compatibility.

### 5. Added Ontology Bonus to Scoring (Section 7.2)

**Added as first item in Supplemental Scores:**
- **Ontology Bonus**: Apply your Ontology card's special effect (see section 4: Ontology Special Effects). This may award bonus points or trigger card draws.

This ensures players remember to check their Ontology effects when scoring.

## Why These Changes Were Made

### 1. Card Counts
- Reflects the actual current state of the game (New Materialism + Practice Theory added)
- Ensures rulebook matches card CSV

### 2. Card Type Details
- Helps players understand the complete card ecosystem
- Shows the distribution of theory cards by alignment
- Makes it easier to understand which cards exist in the game

### 3. Ontology Special Effects
- The cards already had these effects printed on them, but they weren't in the rulebook
- Now players can reference the rules to understand how Ontology effects work
- Balances the three Ontologies with distinct strategic advantages:
  - Realism → favors quantitative/systematic methods
  - Relativism → allows flexibility but caps at +3 paradigm bonus
  - Constructivism → rewards qualitative/interpretive methods with card draw

### 4. Incompatibility Clarification
- Prevents confusion about what "incompatible" means
- Makes it clear that incompatibility is ONLY between Ontology and Epistemology
- Not between Methods and Epistemologies (those are "synergies")

### 5. Ontology Bonus in Scoring
- Ensures the scoring section is complete
- Players won't forget to apply their Ontology effects
- Cross-references the detailed effects in section 4

## Remaining Known Issues
